3 · One-time phone setup

  1. On the your phone, install Syncthing-Fork (Play Store or F-Droid). Make sure the phone's Tailscale is on.
  2. In the app: Devices → ＋ and scan this code (this is your computer). Name it your computer, save.
  3. Within a minute the phone appears below. Tap Accept phone.
  4. The phone then asks to add the folder “Google Photos (from Drive)”. Accept, set the folder path to Pictures/FromDrive and the folder type to Receive only, and save.
  5. Open Google Photos → Library → Photos on device, open each FromDrive folder and turn on Backup. Google Photos backs up per folder.

After Google Photos shows them as backed up, you can use its Free up space on the phone. Your copies here are unaffected.

How this works

One photo at a time. Decide where it should live. Nothing is moved or deleted while you swipe; choices are saved and applied later, after you review the totals.

🖼

Swipe right → Google Photos

Real photos and videos worth having on your phone. Goes into the Google Photos sync folder (one album per folder), and the expansion drive keeps a copy.

📁

Swipe up ↑ Google Drive

Files you want in the cloud but not in your photo timeline: ID scans, certificates, documents, design files, diagrams. Stays in Google Drive, and the expansion drive keeps a copy.

💾

Swipe down ↓ Local only

Keep it, but not in Google at all. Lives only on the expansion drive, and its Drive copy gets removed to free space. Use for old archives, college projects, raw shots.

🗑

Swipe left ← Delete

Not worth keeping anywhere: blurry, duplicates, random screenshots, memes. Goes to a holding folder first (recoverable), never straight to the bin.

Undo: the ↩ Undo button at the top (works again and again, even after closing the page), or Z / Backspace
